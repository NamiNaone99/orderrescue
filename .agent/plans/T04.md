# T04 atomic in-memory booking

## Purpose

Let a dispatcher confirm exactly the submitted stock rows and retry the same confirmation safely. Two competing bookings must never spend the same units. A rejected booking must leave stock, orders, receipts and the state version untouched.

## Surprises & Discoveries

The memory fixture has two distinct eight-unit orders. Home warehouse A has 16 free units, enough to prove one revision increment for two confirmed lines. Warehouse B has eight free units, enough to prove that only one of two distinct orders can win a race.

The request schema can validate the incident SKU's shape before booking, but only the scenario can establish whether that SKU exists. Review and the regression showed that valid stock lines alone were insufficient.
