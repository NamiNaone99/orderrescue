## Decisions and discoveries

Count actual bytes from the body stream and stop at 16,385 bytes. A caller-supplied length may be absent or inaccurate. Preserve JSON parsing and schema validation for accepted bodies. Reuse the established per-instance bucket and trusted-IP resolver, with one shared instance across both handler bundles.
