# T16 — Detect and refuse stale dispatch plans

## Purpose

An operator can trigger the scenario's listed warehouse event, see what changed in the displayed plan, and request a fresh plan. Another operator's change should be detected within one five-second poll. A receipt remains a record of a completed booking as stock continues to update.
