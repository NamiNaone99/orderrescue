# T22 F3 — Ask before planning an incomplete message

## Purpose

An operator who supplies only a SKU must be asked for the missing information even when the model fills every output field. The normal interpreter chain should continue to accept supported warehouse names, named shifts and explicit time ranges.

## Surprises & Discoveries

Existing SKU validation checks the submitted text. Warehouse and time checks validate the values in the output but allow the model to invent both.

## Decision Log

2026-11-21, Codex. Keep grounding after V5 to preserve its retry behaviour. Check the submitted message rather than the quote because the hero quote legitimately omits its warehouse. The model remains responsible for interpreting spoken language.

2026-11-21, Codex. Require a spoken warehouse label at the start or in location context. Thai word boundaries distinguish เอ from เอา. A product containing บี should not provide warehouse evidence. Leave the translation of spoken IDs and times to the model per V2.

## Outcomes & Retrospective

All 20 regressions and 90 interpreter tests passed. The two real-day examples returned incidents over local HTTP. Chrome showed the warehouse clarification for the exact reported case and sent no plan request. The final task log records the broader gates and the limit of text grounding.
