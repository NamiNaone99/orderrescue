# T20. Polish the dispatch desk

21 Nov 2026, Bangkok. Codex local on Windows, GPT-6, Node 22 and pnpm 9.15.9. The expanded user brief calls for projector and phone evidence and a read-only demo guide. The user explicitly confirmed Sarabun for Thai and Latin.

## Production before session

The official Playwright MCP ran the authorised synthetic hero at `orderrescue.vercel.app` on commit `62e6e41957cc9f3f89486f73f5faca4828f67f90`. Health reported Supabase with `degraded:false` before both resets. OpenAI `gpt-6-luna` interpreted the sample on its first attempt in 2,151 ms. First-come showed 1/2 and ฿32. The joint plan showed 2/2 and ฿88. The B event moved readiness from 13:30 to 14:30 and disabled confirmation. Explicit replanning left #101 for the dispatcher and assigned eight units of #102 to B at ฿32. The operator confirmed that row. The booking returned HTTP 201 and receipt `d223f617-e5ab-48d1-b5b1-11f512db142e`. The final hero reset restored two open orders, zero receipts, stock revisions of one and free units A 16, B 8, C 8. [Desktop before](../screenshots/polish-before-desktop.png) and [phone before](../screenshots/polish-before-mobile.png).

## Changes and observations

- Moved each strategy's cost, deadlines, source locations, ready times and order status under its count. The joint gain is attached to the joint strategy. The original detailed comparison stays available for individual deadlines and cost checks.
- Connected the four stages to actual workflow state. Seeded only public scenario metadata for the first paint. An early Analyse click waits for initial inventory and submits once. Incident and plan skeletons preserve the settled geometry.
- The stale banner sits above the dimmed cards and explains the stock change. Its action replans explicitly. A reason accompanies the disabled confirmation. The receipt shows its reference and exports the confirmed rows via clipboard or CSV. Revised stock rows highlight once.
- Added the Thai **วิธีใช้เดโม** dialog with the ฿100 synthetic example, interpreted facts, first-come/joint comparison, booking, and optional B delay. The dialog keeps the draft and current plan. Keyboard and phone checks cover its opening position, focus trap, closure and return focus.
- Hosted four licensed Sarabun weights locally. Thai and Latin glyphs are present in each full font. Lossless WOFF2 encoding used fontTools and Brotli during this task. The checked-in assets require no conversion step during install or build. The upstream copyright and SIL Open Font License accompany the fonts. [Google Fonts Sarabun at the source revision](https://github.com/google/fonts/tree/e774067705989f0bcb5e32eb0e8f76c24d679f92/ofl/sarabun).
- Raised mobile controls to at least 44×44 px, kept reading text at least 14 px and kept one reachable primary action. Reduced-motion mode removes smooth scrolling, highlights and transitions. A genuine local App Router error exposed a same-route recovery link that left the fallback visible. The installed Next 16.4 `retry` callback restored the desk.
