## Frozen simulation

Seed 20261121. The clock is 12:30 and the pre-commit cutoff is 11:00. On hand is 1.10 times demand rounded up. Warehouse shares start at 50/30/20 with seeded jitter of up to ten percentage points. Warehouse A commits pre-cutoff demand capped at on hand. B and C commit a seeded fraction up to 40%. A/B/C next become ready at 13:00/13:30/15:00, with zero or 30 minutes of seeded jitter for B/C. Unit costs are 0/4/7 THB. Deadlines use two-, four- or six-hour leads rounded up to a half-hour and bounded by 13:30–18:00. The default SKU is 23084. The full-day book is known at 12:30.
